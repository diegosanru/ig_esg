Our People

Health and Safety at Industrial Gate

We prioritize the physical integrity and well-being of our employees through pillars that guide our health and safety management.

Safe Work Environment

We prioritize the physical integrity and well-being of all our employees, with clear protocols for prevention and incident response so we can operate in a safe and controlled environment.

Prevention and Regulatory Compliance

We ensure that our labor practices comply with current health and safety regulations, with continuous risk assessment and preventive measures to minimize incidents.

Safety Training

We foster a preventive culture through periodic training programs, where staff acquire practical knowledge to act safely and respond to emergencies.

Comprehensive Health Promotion

Beyond physical safety, we promote comprehensive well-being with initiatives that encourage healthy habits, emotional care, and work-life balance.

Continuous Evaluation and Improvement

We maintain a monitoring and evaluation system that lets us identify opportunities to improve our policies and practices, ensuring their effectiveness and constant updating.

We boost the economy of the communities where we operate, prioritizing the hiring of local suppliers and services, which generates employment and strengthens regional economic activity.

We support initiatives focused on environmental care, ensuring that our social impact is positive and contributes to sustainable development.

We maintain constant communication with authorities to identify needs and cooperation opportunities that benefit both the community and our operation.
