Our key pillars

At Industrial Gate, we understand that sustainability is not an add-on, but rather a strategic pillar for long-term growth. Therefore, we integrate Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) criteria across every stage of our business model, from investment assessment to daily operations, generating lasting value for investors, clients, and communities.

Our environmental commitment is reflected in the development and management of industrial spaces with high energy efficiency, responsible water use, and certifications such as LEED and EDGE. We apply sustainable practices from the design stage of each project, incorporating technology and monitoring that allow us to reduce our environmental footprint and promote resilient infrastructure.

We foster an inclusive, safe, and respectful work environment that supports the professional development of our employees. At the same time, we work with local communities to strengthen the social fabric through investment, job creation, and positive-impact projects in the areas where we operate.

Integrity and transparency are pillars of our operations. We have a strong corporate governance framework, supported by specialized committees, an ESG Policy, and a Code of Ethics that guides our decision-making. Through this framework, we strengthen the trust of our stakeholders and ensure responsible capital management.

Commitment to the Sustainable Development Goals

At Industrial Gate, we recognize that our responsibility goes beyond building high-performance industrial infrastructure; we seek to actively contribute to the sustainable development of the communities where we operate. Therefore, we align our Environmental, Social, and Governance initiatives with the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) established by the UN, identifying those that are directly related to our business strategy and operating model.

We promote equal pay and professional growth for minority groups through inclusion policies.

We implement water use efficiency and recycling systems in our properties.

We ensure dignified working conditions and promote sustainable economic growth.

We build properties under ASG criteria, prioritizing green technologies.

We contribute to the development of sustainable communities through infrastructure projects with a social focus.

We integrate climate change into our investment and asset management decisions.
